隐私摘要：本应用不申请 Internet 权限，不注册账号，不接入广告、统计、推送、云同步或在线模型服务。业务数据仅保存在你的设备本地。

01 / 适用范围

本政策适用于什么

本隐私政策适用于百宝箱DeskHarbor HarmonyOS 应用（以下简称“本应用”）。本应用是一款独立的个人效率与本地工作流工具，与 DeepSeek 官方不存在隶属、合作、授权或背书关系。

本应用的开发者/运营者为应用市场页面公示的实名认证主体。

02 / 数据清单

本应用处理哪些数据

为在本机提供任务管理和工作流功能，本应用会处理：

  • 你主动填写的任务标题、目标、背景、约束、期望产物和标签；
  • 内置、创建或导入的工作流及其输入；
  • 本地模拟引擎产生的会话事件、检查点、运行状态与产物；
  • 主题、减少动效、执行速度等偏好设置，以及协议同意状态。

本应用不会主动收集手机号、位置、通讯录、设备标识、广告标识或账号信息。你可能在任务正文中自行输入个人信息，因此请将任务内容视为私密数据并妥善管理。

03 / 存储

数据存储位置与保留期限

上述数据保存在设备本地的应用沙箱内，包括本地数据库、偏好设置和沙箱文件，不与任何服务器通信。

任务及关联运行数据保留至你删除对应任务、清空业务数据或卸载应用为止；偏好设置和协议状态保留至你恢复初始状态或卸载应用为止。系统备份行为如适用，受设备和操作系统设置控制。

04 / 权限

权限、导入与导出

当前版本不申请网络、相机、麦克风、位置、通讯录、通知或后台运行权限，也不申请宽泛存储权限。

导入和导出仅在你主动操作时通过系统文件选择器访问你选择的文件或位置；复制和系统分享也只在你主动点击时触发。导出的文件可能包含你输入的私密内容，请自行选择安全的保存位置和接收方。

05 / 第三方

数据共享、转让与第三方 SDK

本应用不向开发者服务器或第三方上传、共享、出售或转让你的任务和工作流数据，不包含广告、统计、账号、在线模型、支付或推送 SDK，也不进行跨境数据传输。

若未来版本新增联网能力、第三方服务或数据处理目的，我们会先更新本政策，并在依法需要时重新征得你的同意。

06 / 用户控制

你如何管理自己的数据

你可以在应用内查看、编辑、导出和删除任务及工作流；在“我的 → 数据管理”中清空全部业务数据或恢复初始状态；也可以撤销隐私政策同意。撤销后应用返回协议页面，但不会自动删除已有本地业务数据，你可另行执行清空。

卸载应用通常会删除其本地沙箱数据。若你对数据处理有疑问或希望行使相关权利，也可以通过本政策所列邮箱联系我们。

07 / 安全

本地安全措施

本应用依赖 HarmonyOS 应用沙箱隔离数据；工作流不允许执行脚本、系统命令、网络请求或未注册工具；导入内容会经过大小、结构和工具白名单校验；诊断日志不记录任务正文、产物内容、外部文件内容或完整绝对路径。

任何技术措施都无法保证绝对安全。请为设备设置可靠的锁屏保护，并在导出敏感内容后妥善保管文件。

08 / 未成年人

未成年人保护

本产品主要面向知识工作者与开发者，不以未成年人为目标用户，也不通过年龄信息识别用户。监护人如发现未成年人输入了不适当信息，可通过应用内删除、清空数据或卸载应用进行处理，并可联系我们。

09 / 更新

政策如何更新

当产品功能、处理目的、数据类型、权限、第三方 SDK 或适用规则发生实质变化时，我们会更新版本和生效日期，并在应用内或公开页面显著提示；依法需要重新同意时，在你同意前不会进入核心功能。

10 / 联系

联系我们

开发者/运营者：以应用市场公示的实名认证主体为准。

如对本政策、个人信息处理方式或权利请求有疑问、建议或投诉，请发送邮件至 tomkuku588@gmail.com。请勿在邮件中发送身份证件、密码或与问题无关的敏感信息。

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how 百宝箱DeskHarbor handles data locally on your device and how you can control that data.

Privacy summary: The app does not request Internet permission and has no accounts, advertising, analytics, push notifications, cloud sync, or online model services. Business data stays on your device.

01 / Scope

What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the 百宝箱DeskHarbor HarmonyOS app (the “App”). The App is an independent personal productivity and local workflow tool. It is not affiliated with, partnered with, authorized by, or endorsed by DeepSeek.

The developer/operator is the verified identity displayed on the applicable app marketplace listing.

02 / Data inventory

Data handled by the App

To provide task and workflow functions on your device, the App handles:

  • Task titles, goals, background, constraints, expected outputs, and tags that you enter;
  • Built-in, created, or imported workflows and their inputs;
  • Session events, checkpoints, run states, and artifacts produced by the local simulation engine;
  • Preferences such as theme, reduced motion, and execution speed, plus your agreement status.

The App does not actively collect phone numbers, location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or account information. You may choose to enter personal information in task text, so treat task content as private and manage it carefully.

03 / Storage

Storage location and retention

This data is stored in the App's local on-device sandbox, including its local database, preferences, and sandbox files. It is not transmitted to any server.

Tasks and related run data remain until you delete the task, clear business data, or uninstall the App. Preferences and agreement status remain until you restore the initial state or uninstall the App. Where applicable, operating-system backups are controlled by your device and system settings.

04 / Permissions

Permissions, import, and export

The current version does not request network, camera, microphone, location, contacts, notifications, background operation, or broad storage permissions.

Import and export use the system file picker only after you initiate the action and only for the file or location you select. Copy and system sharing are also triggered only by your action. Exported files may contain private content you entered; choose secure storage locations and recipients.

05 / Third parties

Sharing, transfer, and third-party SDKs

The App does not upload, share, sell, or transfer your task or workflow data to developer servers or third parties. It contains no advertising, analytics, account, online model, payment, or push SDKs and performs no cross-border data transfers.

If a future version adds connectivity, third-party services, or new processing purposes, we will update this policy first and obtain renewed consent where required by law.

06 / User control

How you control your data

You can view, edit, export, and delete tasks and workflows in the App. Under “Me → Data Management,” you can clear all business data or restore the initial state. You can also withdraw consent to this policy. Withdrawal returns the App to the agreement page but does not automatically delete existing local business data; you can clear it separately.

Uninstalling the App will normally remove its local sandbox data. You may also contact us at the address below with questions or requests concerning data handling.

07 / Security

Local security measures

The App relies on the HarmonyOS application sandbox for isolation. Workflows cannot execute scripts, system commands, network requests, or unregistered tools. Imported content is checked for size, structure, and allowed tools. Diagnostic logs do not record task text, artifact content, external file content, or full absolute paths.

No technical measure can provide absolute security. Use a reliable device lock and safeguard exported files that contain sensitive content.

08 / Children

Children's privacy

The product is primarily intended for knowledge workers and developers. It is not directed at children and does not identify users by age. If a guardian discovers that a child entered inappropriate information, they may delete it, clear App data, uninstall the App, or contact us.

09 / Updates

Changes to this policy

If product functions, processing purposes, data types, permissions, third-party SDKs, or applicable rules materially change, we will update the version and effective date and provide a prominent notice in the App or on this public page. Where renewed consent is legally required, core functions will remain unavailable until you agree.

10 / Contact

Contact us

Developer/operator: the verified identity displayed on the applicable app marketplace listing.

For questions, suggestions, complaints, or rights requests related to this policy or personal information handling, email tomkuku588@gmail.com. Do not include identity documents, passwords, or unrelated sensitive information.
